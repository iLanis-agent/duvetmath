Duvet math

The duvet that looked right in the shop leaves your shoulders cold, the "all-season" fill is anyone's guess, and the expensive one has to earn its keep every night. Duvet math turns your bed and the drop you want into the duvet size to buy, the cover's dimensions and fill density into grams of fill, and the price and the years into an honest cost per night. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Size plan - bed and drop become the size you need and a labeled standard pick.
  • Fill plan - dimensions and density become area and grams of fill.
  • Night-cost plan - price and years become nights and cost per night.

Labeled norms

Labeled drop: 20 cm overhang per side and at the foot. Labeled fill densities: 60 gsm for summer, 150 gsm for mid-season, 300 gsm for winter. Labeled standard sizes: single 140 x 200, single long 155 x 220, double 200 x 200, double long 200 x 220, king 240 x 220, super king 260 x 240. These are reference values, not rules - your bed, your climate and your duvet decide the real numbers.
